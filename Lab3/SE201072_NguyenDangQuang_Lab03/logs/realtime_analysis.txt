ANALYSIS
The measured maximum latency increased under system stress.
Maximum latency is important because a large delay may cause
a real-time task to miss its deadline.

PREEMPT_RT COMPARISON
PREEMPT_RT is designed to improve scheduling predictability
and reduce worst-case latency.

The current Ubuntu kernel does not enable PREEMPT_RT.
Therefore, no experimental PREEMPT_RT measurements
were performed in this lab.

CONCLUSION
PREEMPT_RT is generally more suitable for applications
requiring strict timing constraints, such as robot control.
However, meeting a specific deadline must be verified
through testing under realistic worst-case conditions.
